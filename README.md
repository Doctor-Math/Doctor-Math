# 👾 **About Me**
Hello! I am a **Data Science student at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)**. I previously served as **Project Director at Pi Júnior**, where I led analytics, modeling, and data-driven solutions to transform uncertainty into strategic decisions.
